📖 How to Play & Rules

🎯 The Golden Rule: Complete Cooperation

Neither Ember nor Tide can finish the level alone! One player must step on pressure plates, activate levers, or charge elemental runes to open routes for the other.

🔥 Lava & Water Hazards 💧

• Lava (Orange): Safe for Ember. Deadly to Tide.
• Water (Blue): Safe for Tide. Deadly to Ember.
• Toxic Acid (Green) & Spikes (Grey): Deadly to both characters!

⚙️ Puzzles & Mechanisms

• Pressure Plates: Step on them to open doors or move elevators.
• Elemental Runes: Stand next to your matching elemental rune to charge ancient locks.
• Dual Switches: Both players must hit their corresponding buttons within 2.5 seconds!
• Checkpoints: Reach glowing shrines to save your checkpoint if either player falls.

🚪 The Gateway to Victory

Both Ember and Tide must step into their respective portal doors (🔥 Fire Arch & 💧 Water Arch) to activate the exit and advance to the next level!
